## Monthly Growth Status metric
- [x] Add one metric card labelled `Monthly Growth Status` showing one exact status: `Business is Growing` in green, `Business is Stable` in yellow, `Business is Declining` in red, or `Business is Volatile` in purple. Derive the status from sales data and make the comparison period/interpretation clear so a demo result is not mistaken for a live business guarantee. The demo compares month-to-date revenue with the same elapsed days across the current and previous two months (using the common available day if a month is shorter), preferring recorded sale revenue and otherwise using quantity × unit price. Growing is at least +5%; declining is at most −5%; the band between is stable. Volatile takes priority when two consecutive comparable-period changes reverse direction and each is at least 10% in magnitude. Missing comparison history displays Stable with an insufficient-history note.
